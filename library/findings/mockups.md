# Findings: mockups.digital

Source: https://mockups.digital/  
IDs: F-184 to F-248 (65)  
Prints: `prints/mock-*.webp`

Photo mockups, no motion: one still per item. F-184 to F-190 are the thumbnail grids of the 7 categories (262 grid prints between them). Opened item by item: representatives of Device and paper/desk items of Stationery. Frame, Outdoor, Packing, Apparel and Vehicle exist only as grids.

Snapshot collected on 2026-09-22. Catalogs change; recount before trusting totals. Findings are factual descriptions: `what it has` and `motion`.

---

## F-184 · mockups.digital · Device Category (grid)
- link: https://mockups.digital/category/device
- prints: mock-grid-device-01.webp ... -54.webp (whole grid scrolled, 54 prints)
- what it has: white background, 3-column grid, each card with a square photo + category + Free/paid badge + product
  name below. 267 mockups: mostly iPhone (several generations, colors and angles of the same model repeated), MacBook
  Pro/Air, iPad/iPad Pro, Apple Watch, Studio Display/Pro Display XDR, Mac Pro, iMac, and a few non-Apple (Samsung
  Galaxy, Pixel, folding Huawei). Photography varies between studio still (plain background, soft shadow) and real-life
  scene (hand holding it, wooden table, sofa).
- motion: static grid, no per-item animation; the only "sequence" is scrolling to list everything.
