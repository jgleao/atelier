## Scroll demos (captured at 6 scroll positions each)

1. **Scroll (hero)**: "GSAP allows you to create jaw dropping scroll effects with minimal code for maximum impact." Pill/circle shapes with a pink/purple/blue gradient floating.
2. **Scroll Plugins**: "Infinitely Flexible, Highly Optimised (...) Slide, zoom, morph, draw. Link any GSAP animation to scroll, it's completely flexible."
3. **ScrollTrigger, Ready Set Scroll**: "Tell stunning and interactive stories. Give any tween or timeline a ScrollTrigger with just one line of code or customise as needed." (element called "worm" in the code, a worm/snake-like shape).
4. **Pin, Scrub, Debug**: "Pin sections in place, scrub through animations with the scrollbar, and debug easily by enabling visual markers." (element called "windmill" in the code, a windmill).
5. **Any Size**: "Craft animations for any viewport size with gsap.matchMedia()."
6. **ScrollSmoother, gentle breeze**: "It's like a gentle breeze."
7. **Effortless**: "Effortlessly guiding your users from one section to another."
8. **Smooth like butter**: "ScrollSmoother leverages the native scroll mechanics meaning you can deliver a fluid and immersive experience for everyone, no matter the device or user input."
9. **Mesmerising effects (parallax)**: "Add gorgeous parallax and trailing effects by simply adding a data attribute" (shows `data-speed="0.8"`, `2.0`, `1.2` across layers).
10. **Seamlessly integrated**: "Get all the capabilities of GSAP and ScrollTrigger with the added benefits of smooth scrolling."
11. **Back to Basics with Observer**: "Need more low-level control? Easily detect scroll and other events without wrestling with implementation details."
